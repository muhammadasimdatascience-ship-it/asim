About me

I am a master’s student at Guizhou University, China, working in the AI for Science lab at the State Key Laboratory of Public Big Data, School of Big Data and Engineering. My research interests center on applying machine learning to scientific problems, with a particular interest in molecular and peptide design.

I hold a BS in Data Science from Muhammad Nawaz Sharif University of Agriculture, Multan, Pakistan. My background spans machine learning, computer vision, and data analysis, and I have co-authored journal articles on visual recognition and QR-code security.

I am interested in research collaborations at the intersection of artificial intelligence and scientific discovery.
